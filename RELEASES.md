# Release naming

Every update ships as `Pocket-City-vMAJOR.MINOR.PATCH-GitHub.zip`.

- **PATCH**: fixes and small refinements, such as `1.2.1`.
- **MINOR**: new features, such as `1.2.0`.
- **MAJOR**: major game revisions, such as `2.0.0`.

Keep previous versioned ZIPs unchanged. Upload the extracted files, not the ZIP, when updating a GitHub repository.

## 1.2.0 — Big World Update

Adds the nine-district world, atlas fast travel, 50 new stars, Moonbase relay repair, Prism Canyon crystal hunting, Gigglecap rescues, Neon hoverboard racing, and the Cloudtop sky beacon. The existing `pocketCity-v1` save format remains compatible.

## 1.1.0 — Dash & Discover

Added faster fixed-step movement, sprint, dash, double jump, grapple improvements, bubble hold-to-fire, super bounce chaining, star streak boosts, City Sprint, pooled effects, and grouped scenery.
